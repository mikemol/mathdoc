Enrich the report, keeping the existing level of detail as a baseline.

Enrich the report such that each section provides the greatest structural leverage to the next section, each subsection provides the greatest structural leverage to the next subsection, and so on.

Enrich the report such that each section enriches and refines the next and previous sections, each subsection enriches and refines the next and previous subsections, and so on.

Maintain a degree of utility suitable for introductory up through graduate-level reference.

Apply the CHIP N+1 as a playbook for constructing the enriched report (don't mention CHIP N+1, just use it)
